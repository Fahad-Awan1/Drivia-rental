Our story

Built by people who love the road.

Our founders spent years renting cars for work and holidays, and kept running into the same problems: confusing add-ons, long counters and cars that didn’t match the photo.

So we built the rental company we wanted to use: every car hand-picked and under three years old, every price shown upfront, and real people available around the clock.

The road so far

From a single garage to four continents, here are the milestones.

  1. 2018

    First keys

    Launched in Dubai Marina with four cars and a WhatsApp booking line.

  2. 2020

    London & digital keys

    Opened in Mayfair and went fully contactless with phone-as-key pickup.

  3. 2022

    Riviera season

    Milan and Nice branches launched with one-way trips across the Alps.

  4. 2024

    Coast to coast

    Miami and Los Angeles opened, and EVs reached 30% of the fleet.

  5. 2026

    2,500+ drivers

    Launched the 3D showroom and our always-on Drivia assistant.
